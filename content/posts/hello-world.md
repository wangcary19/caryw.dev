Welcome. This is my corner of the internet — a place for short notes,
project updates, and links.

## What's here

- **Writing** — occasional essays and notes. Some are also published on
  [Substack](https://carywang.substack.com).
- **Links** — the usual suspects: GitHub, LinkedIn, email, and resume.

## How this site works

It's built with [Next.js](https://nextjs.org), deployed on Vercel, and the
background is a blue haze that follows your cursor. Posts are plain Markdown
files, which keeps everything fast and easy to edit.

Thanks for stopping by.
