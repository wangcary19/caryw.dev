---
title: "Why I write"
date: "2026-03-02"
description: "Writing is thinking with the receipts attached."
---

I write because writing is the fastest way to find out what I actually
think. An idea that feels clear in my head usually isn't — it's just
familiar. Putting it into sentences forces it to stand on its own.

Some notes:

- **Short posts beat long ones.** If I can't say it in a page, I don't
  understand it yet.
- **Publish, then fix.** A published post with a typo beats a perfect
  draft that never ships.
- **Links outlive feeds.** A URL I control is the only archive I can
  count on.

That last point is a big reason this site exists at all.
